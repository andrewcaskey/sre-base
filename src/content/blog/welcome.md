I run production systems for a living — monitoring them, instrumenting them, and
being the person who gets paged when they misbehave. Along the way I accumulate
a lot of small, hard-won lessons that never make it into a runbook or a ticket.

This is where those go.

## What you'll find here

- **Observability** — tracing, metrics, logs, and the glue between them.
  OpenTelemetry, Dynatrace, Prometheus, and the pipelines that feed them.
- **Infrastructure** — containers, namespaces, networking, and the layers
  underneath the abstractions we lean on every day.
- **Reliability** — SLOs, incident response, and the operational habits that
  keep systems boring in the best way.

## Why a separate site

My [portfolio](https://andrewcaskey.com) is the highlight reel. This is the
lab notebook — rougher, more frequent, and written for the next engineer trying
to understand *why*, not just *what*.

More soon.
